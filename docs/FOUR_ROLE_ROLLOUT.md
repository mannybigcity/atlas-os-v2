# ATLAS Four-Role Workflow Rollout

This plan turns the named ATLAS team into real, limited, auditable workflows.
The names are not decorative characters, and they are not a promise of four
fully autonomous employees on day one.

## ATLAS — Chief of Staff

ATLAS is the operating layer. It keeps the client's goal, priorities, work,
approvals, and follow-up visible in one place. As the other workflows come
online, ATLAS will route handoffs, prepare a daily briefing, surface blocked
work, and keep a permanent activity record.

Done means priorities and handoffs are stored, every external action has an
owner and status, and the client can see what happened and approve what is next.

## HUNTER — Lead Research

HUNTER will take an approved customer profile, geography, and search criteria;
query approved sources such as Google Places; organize and deduplicate possible
leads; and place them in a review queue. HUNTER will not scrape restricted data
or contact a lead automatically.

Done means API permissions and cost limits are configured, source and search
criteria are recorded, duplicates are handled, and Manny approves leads before
they enter outreach or follow-up.

## MICAH — Content Studio

MICAH will use an approved brand profile and content brief to create draft
graphics, captions, and a publishing schedule. The client or Manny reviews the
draft before anything is posted. Direct publishing comes later, only after each
platform's OAuth permissions and approval controls are tested.

Done means every draft has a source brief, brand reference, review status, and
record of who approved it.

## DAVID — CRM and Follow-Up

DAVID will organize contacts, pipeline stages, next steps, reminders, and draft
follow-up messages. DAVID will protect consent and contact history and will not
send unsolicited or unapproved messages.

Done means each contact has an owner, stage, source, consent basis, next action,
and complete follow-up history.

## Delivery sequence

1. Business Assessment and Lion's Den lead queue.
2. ATLAS operating view: priorities, approvals, and daily briefing.
3. HUNTER research workflow using approved APIs and strict cost controls.
4. DAVID contact pipeline and follow-up queue.
5. MICAH branded draft creation and approval queue.
6. Optional publishing or outbound actions after permissions, compliance, and
   human approval are proven.

## Commercial boundary

The $49 founding membership covers early access to the core workspace,
onboarding, the focused plan, work review, check-ins, and staged access to the
approved workflows. Third-party API usage, paid data, advertising spend,
production printing, and custom high-volume work must be scoped separately.
