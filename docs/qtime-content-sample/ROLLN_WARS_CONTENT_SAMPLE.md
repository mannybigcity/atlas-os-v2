# QTime Productions — Roll'n Wars Content Sample

Prepared for QTime Productions on July 18, 2026.

## What Atlas reviewed

This sample is based on QTime's public-facing material and the QTime business profile supplied by Quincy. Public sources reviewed:

- QTime Productions on Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/qtimeproductions/
- QTime promotional-video example: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FU1xz3qEi0M
- Food4Thought Network: https://www.youtube.com/@Food4Thoughtnetwork
- Roll'n Wars listing at New Terrain Brewing: https://newterrainbrewing.com/events

The public material consistently uses short, high-energy language, head-to-head competition, crowd voting, event promotion, and direct calls to follow or participate. QTime's black-and-white production identity is recognizable, while Roll'n Wars can support a stronger campaign-specific color system.

## Suggested first-week content sequence

1. Post the selected hero flyer and ask followers which food category they want to see battle next.
2. Publish a competitor-reveal card for Truck A.
3. Publish a competitor-reveal card for Truck B.
4. Post a short host video explaining how crowd voting works.
5. Publish the confirmed date, location, and voting instructions.
6. Share behind-the-scenes preparation from both competitors.
7. Post the result, winner reaction, and next-battle teaser.

## Approval checklist before anything is published

- Select one visual direction.
- Confirm the official spelling and treatment of the Roll'n Wars and QTime names.
- Replace concept food and truck imagery with approved competitor photography when available.
- Confirm the next battle date, time, venue, and participating trucks.
- Confirm whether the call to action should be `Comment BATTLE`, direct message, phone, website, or an application form.
- Obtain permission for every competitor, venue, sponsor, logo, and customer image used.

## Important concept note

These are deliberately labeled draft concepts. The pictured trucks, dishes, crowd, and event setting are illustrative—not representations of a particular competitor or completed QTime event. No unconfirmed date, venue, sponsor, price, result, or performance claim has been added.
