## Suggested first message structure

Do not pitch “AI agents.” Lead with one business outcome and a question:

> Hi [Name] — I run Atlas For Entrepreneurs here in the Cypress/Houston area.
> I help owner-led businesses keep inquiries, follow-up, and practical marketing
> from getting lost when the business gets busy. I noticed [one verified public
> fact]. Would it be useful if I prepared a no-cost snapshot of where a lead or
> repeat-customer opportunity may be slipping through? No obligation.

Personalize the bracketed fact for every business. Do not mass-send this text.
